How to use it

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Y, or click the toolbar icon
  2. The panel appears bottom-right with the result, already ready to copy
  3. Esc closes it

This page opens by itself the first time the extension is installed. That is deliberate: nobody discovers a right-click menu or a keyboard shortcut on their own, and a feature nobody finds may as well not exist.

Settings

Everything stays in this browser profile. Nothing is uploaded, because this extension has no network permission — check the permissions row under chrome://extensions yourself.
